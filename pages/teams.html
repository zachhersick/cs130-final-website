Factory Programs

Each factory invests in testing, engine mapping, and suspension support that can swing lap times by tenths. Here is how the 2025 squads line up.

KTM Factory Racing

Riders: Cooper Webb, Aaron Plessinger.

Focus: Refined chassis flex and new shock valving for better whoop drive.

Star Racing Yamaha

Riders: Eli Tomac, Haiden Deegan.

Focus: Strong engines with a reputation for holeshots; lighter swingarm to calm rear-end chatter.

Honda HRC

Riders: Chase Sexton, Jett Lawrence.

Focus: Corner speed and updated ECU maps that smooth throttle response exiting slick bowls.

Kawasaki Racing Team

Riders: Jason Anderson, Adam Cianciarulo.

Focus: Engine braking adjustments to keep the KX planted on rutted downhills.

GasGas Factory

Riders: Justin Barcia, Pierce Brown.

Focus: Aggressive chassis feel; aims to convert Barcia’s strong starts into main-event calmness.

Suzuki RM-Z Program

Riders: Ken Roczen.

Focus: Stability-first setup with tried-and-true components; betting on starts and consistency.

Why Teams Matter

  • Factory suspension trucks re-valve forks between sessions to match rut depth.
  • Data engineers monitor engine temps and traction control to keep power delivery predictable.
  • Top programs assign dedicated practice starts coaches to trim tenths off the launch.
