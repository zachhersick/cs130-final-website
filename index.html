About Supercross

Supercross is a form of off-road motorcycle racing held on artificially made dirt tracks consisting of steep jumps, obstacles, tight turns, and rhythm sections. Races are typically contested in stadiums and attract top riders from around the world.

This site covers the sport broadly — from rules and bike classes (250cc and 450cc) to teams, riders, and seasonal coverage. Use the navigation to explore schedules, rider lineups, team information, and season analysis. For details about the current season, see the dedicated 2025 Season page.
